# Sushi Tower — Privacy Policy


**Last updated: September 5, 2026**

Sushi Tower is an idle restaurant game published by Cat Nap. This policy
explains what the app collects, who it goes to, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

Your game progress stays on your phone. The app shows ads, and the ad network
sees your device's advertising ID unless you tell it not to. The app can also
send anonymous gameplay statistics — how far up the tower people get, which
upgrades they buy — that are not tied to you or to your device.

## What stays on your device

Your tower, your coins, your gems, your upgrades, your settings and whether you
have bought anything are stored in a file on your device. They are not uploaded
anywhere, are not backed up to us, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What we collect

Sushi Tower is built to send anonymous gameplay events to our own server so we
can tell whether the game is too slow, where people stop playing, and whether
anything is broken. These events describe **the game, not you**:

- which floor was built and how long after installing;
- which upgrades were bought and at what level;
- when a boost was started, and whether it came from a video or from gems;
- how long you were away, and what the catch-up paid;
- which ads were offered, shown, skipped or watched, and why one was not shown;
- whether an in-app purchase succeeded, and which product it was for.

These events carry a **session identifier that is a random number**, created
when you start playing and forgotten after 30 minutes of inactivity. They do
**not** carry your advertising ID, your device ID, your name, your email, your
location or your IP address as an identifier. There is no account and nothing to
log in to, so there is nothing here that identifies you and consequently nothing
we could look up, export or delete if you asked us to.

**Today, none of this is sent anywhere.** The collection endpoint is not
configured in the released app, so these events are recorded in memory on your
own device and discarded when you close the game. If that changes, it changes in
a new version of the app, and the date at the top of this policy changes with
it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sushi Tower is free and is paid for by ads, served by Google AdMob. Google
receives, and processes under its own privacy policy:

- your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier);
- your **approximate location**, derived from your IP address;
- information about **the ads you were shown and whether you interacted with
  them**.

Google uses this to choose which ads to show and to detect fraud. Google's
policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choice.** Where the law requires it — the EEA, the UK and Switzerland —
you are asked, when you first open the game, whether ads may be personalised
using your advertising ID. The dialog is Google's own consent form.

- **If you agree**, ads may be personalised.
- **If you refuse**, you still see ads and you can still play everything, but the
  ads are **not** personalised: Google is told not to use your advertising ID for
  targeting.

You can change your mind at any time from your device's settings, where you can
also reset or delete your advertising ID entirely (**Settings → Google → Ads** on
most Android devices).

Rewarded videos — the ones you choose to watch to double your money, speed up a
build, or take a boost — are always optional. Nothing in this game is locked
behind one, and there is no banner advertising anywhere in it.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Sushi Tower offers three in-app purchases: "Remove ads", and two packs of gems.
Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment
details. Google tells the app what has been bought, and the app remembers that
locally.

"Remove ads" removes the occasional full-screen ad shown when you close the shop
or the map. **Rewarded videos remain available** — they are the ones you choose
to watch in exchange for something, and removing them would take a feature away
rather than give one. The purchase screen says so in those words before you buy.

The two gem packs are consumable: you can buy the same pack again. Gems are
spent inside the game on cosmetic items and on skipping build timers. They
cannot be exchanged for money or for anything outside the game, and nothing in
the game gambles them.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own terms.

## Children

Sushi Tower is not directed at children and is rated for ages 13 and over. We do
not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has
provided us with information, contact us at the address below and we will remove
it.

## Data retention

Gameplay events, if and when they are collected, are kept for 12 months and then
deleted. Ad data is retained by Google under its own policy.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material
change will be announced in the app's store listing.
