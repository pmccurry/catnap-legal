# Sortie — Privacy Policy


**Last updated: September 3, 2026**

Sortie is a ball-sorting puzzle game published by Cat Nap. This policy
explains what the app collects, who it goes to, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

Your game progress stays on your phone. The app shows ads, and the ad network
sees your device's advertising ID unless you tell it not to. The app also sends
anonymous gameplay statistics — which levels are hard, which buttons get used —
that are not tied to you or to your device.

## What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, boosters, settings, chosen character and the level you are
part-way through are stored in a file on your device. They are not uploaded
anywhere, are not backed up to us, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What we collect

Sortie sends anonymous gameplay events to our own server so we can tell which
levels are too hard and which parts of the game are not working. These events
describe **the game, not you**:

- which level was started, finished, restarted or abandoned, how many moves it
  took, how long it took, how many attempts;
- which boosters and hints were used, and whether they were paid for with coins
  or with a video;
- which ads were shown, skipped or watched;
- your coin balance and streak at the moment a level ends.

These events carry a **session identifier that is a random number**, created when
you start playing and forgotten after 30 minutes of inactivity. They do **not**
carry your advertising ID, your device ID, your name, your email, your location
or your IP address as an identifier. There is no account and nothing to log in
to, so there is nothing here that identifies you and consequently nothing we
could look up, export or delete if you asked us to.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sortie is free and is paid for by ads, served by Google AdMob. Google receives,
and processes under its own privacy policy:

- your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier);
- your **approximate location**, derived from your IP address;
- information about **the ads you were shown and whether you interacted with
  them**.

Google uses this to choose which ads to show and to detect fraud. Google's
policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choice.** Where the law requires it — the EEA, the UK and Switzerland —
you are asked, when you first open the game, whether ads may be personalised
using your advertising ID. The dialog is Google's own consent form.

- **If you agree**, ads may be personalised.
- **If you refuse**, you still see ads and you can still play everything, but the
  ads are **not** personalised: Google is told not to use your advertising ID for
  targeting.

You can change your mind at any time from your device's settings, where you can
also reset or delete your advertising ID entirely (**Settings → Google → Ads** on
most Android devices).

Rewarded videos — the ones you choose to watch for a booster or to double your
coins — are always optional. Nothing in the game is locked behind one.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Sortie offers one in-app purchase, "Remove ads". The purchase is handled entirely
by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Google tells the app whether
the purchase is owned, and the app remembers that locally so it can stop showing
you ads between levels.

"Remove ads" removes the ads shown between levels. **Rewarded videos remain
available** — they are the ones you choose to watch in exchange for a booster,
and removing them would take a feature away rather than give one.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own terms.

## Children

Sortie is not directed at children and is rated for ages 13 and over. We do not
knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided
us with information, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

## Data retention

Gameplay events are kept for 12 months and then deleted.
Ad data is retained by Google under its own policy.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material
change will be announced in the app's store listing.
